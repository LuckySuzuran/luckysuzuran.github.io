Some of my favorite moments in a game happen between objectives: a quiet path, a patch of light, or the sound of water when there is nothing urgent to do.

This is an **illustrative review**, with an original landscape and a small animated scene. It is also a working example of the image and video components you can use in your own posts.

## Taking the scenic route

I like worlds that leave a little room for wandering. A place feels memorable when I can recognize its shape before looking at the map: a pine tree on a ridge, a narrow bridge, a mountain beyond the lake.

![An illustrated mountain lake with pine trees and a warm setting sun]({{ '/assets/images/quiet-landscape.svg' | relative_url }})

The illustration above uses ordinary Markdown image syntax. Click it to zoom in, or focus it with the keyboard and press Enter. Press Escape to return.

### The details I notice

- **A clear silhouette.** A few shapes can suggest an entire place.
- **A slower rhythm.** Quiet stretches make the next discovery feel earned.
- **A reason to stop.** Not every view needs a reward attached.

> Sometimes the best side quest is deciding to stay here a little longer.

## A note to future me

Keep a few screenshots. Write down what made the evening good. A short paragraph while the memory is fresh is often worth more than a perfect review that never gets written.

And sometimes, close the game and go for a walk.
